## Description
Take control of your 2026 fantasy football season with Fantasy FB Draft Kit!

Going to a live fantasy football draft? Leave the paper, pens, and draft books at home. Fantasy FB Draft Kit gives you the tools you need to prepare for your draft, track every pick, build your team, and stay informed throughout the season—all from your iPhone or iPad.

DRAFT RANKINGS\
• Overall Top 300\
• Top 50 Rookies\
• Rankings for QB, RB, WR, TE, K, and Defense\
• STD, PPR, and Half-PPR scoring formats\
• Player bye weeks and team information

DRAFT TOOLS\
• Add players to your fantasy team as you draft\
• Mark players as drafted as other managers make their picks\
• Quickly see which players are still available\
• Add unlisted players when needed\
• Keep your original draft results for reference

SEASON TOOLS\
• NFL depth charts\
• Player injury reports and status updates\
• Fantasy football news\
• Fantasy leaders\
• Start/Sit Assistant

MANAGE YOUR TEAM
Keep using Fantasy FB Draft Kit after draft day. Manage your fantasy rosters by adding and dropping players while keeping your original draft picks available for reference.

TRACK YOUR FANTASY HISTORY
Manage multiple fantasy leagues and revisit previous seasons so your fantasy football history stays with you year after year.

From draft preparation to in-season management, Fantasy FB Draft Kit gives you one place to manage your fantasy football season.

Unlisted Player values accepted can be found at [https://github.com/japio7/iosApps/blob/master/Fantasy_FB_Draft_Kit/Unlisted_Player_Values](https://github.com/japio7/iosApps/blob/master/Fantasy_FB_Draft_Kit/Unlisted%20Player%20Values%20Accepted.pdf)
